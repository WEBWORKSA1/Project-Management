Frequently asked questions

Are the tools really free?

Yes. Every tool on Project.Management runs in your browser with no account, no trial and no credit card. The site is funded by advertising, sponsorships and voluntary support.

Where is my project data stored?

Tool data stays in your own browser (local storage) on your device. We never upload what you type into a tool. You can export to CSV or print to PDF at any time.

Can I use the templates at work?

Yes. Templates and tool exports may be used for personal and commercial projects. Please do not resell the templates themselves.

Which methodology should I use?

It depends on how stable your requirements are, how often you can ship, and your governance needs. Take our 2-minute Methodology Quiz for a scored recommendation.

Can you recommend project management software?

Yes. Use the Get Expert Help form and we will send a vendor-neutral shortlist matched to your team size, budget and workflow within 48 hours.
