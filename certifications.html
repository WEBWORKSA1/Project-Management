How to choose the right certification

  • New to project management? Start with the Google certificate, CAPM® or PRINCE2® Foundation to learn the vocabulary.
  • 3+ years leading projects? The PMP® is usually the highest-return credential, especially in North America and the Middle East.
  • Working in agile teams? PSM I or CSM first; PMI-ACP® once you have agile experience across several approaches.
  • UK, Europe, Australia or government? PRINCE2® is frequently requested; APM qualifications lead toward Chartered Project Professional status in the UK.
  • IT generalist? CompTIA Project+ is a solid, lifetime credential.

A 10-week PMP® study plan (outline)

  1. Weeks 1–2: complete your 35 hours of training; read the exam content outline.
  2. Weeks 3–5: people domain and agile/hybrid practices.
  3. Weeks 6–7: process domain — scope, schedule, cost, quality, risk, procurement.
  4. Week 8: business environment, compliance, benefits realisation.
  5. Week 9: full-length timed mock exams; review every wrong answer.
  6. Week 10: targeted revision, rest before exam day.

Frequently asked questions

Which project management certification is best for beginners?

Entry-level options include the CAPM®, the Google Project Management Certificate, CompTIA Project+ and PRINCE2® Foundation. None require prior project leadership experience.

Is the PMP worth it?

For experienced project managers, the PMP® is the most widely recognised general PM credential, frequently listed in job ads and associated with higher salaries in PMI's own surveys. It requires significant experience and study time.

PRINCE2 or PMP?

PRINCE2® is a process-based method popular in the UK, Europe, Australia and public sector. PMP® is a broader experience-based credential recognised globally and especially in North America. Many senior PMs eventually hold both.

PSM or CSM?

Both are entry Scrum Master credentials. PSM I requires passing a rigorous online assessment with no mandatory course and does not expire; CSM requires a two-day course and periodic renewal.
