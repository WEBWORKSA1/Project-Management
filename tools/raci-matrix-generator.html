RACI Matrix Generator

Click a cell to cycle through R, A, C, I and A/R. The validator flags missing or duplicate Accountables, unowned work and overloaded people as you go.

Key: R Responsible · A Accountable · C Consulted · I Informed · A/R both.

Five RACI rules that prevent confusion

  1. Exactly one A per row. Shared accountability means no accountability.
  2. At least one R per row. Someone must actually do the work.
  3. Keep C small. Every Consulted person adds a review cycle.
  4. Use roles, not names, for reusable templates; add names in the project version.
  5. Review it at kickoff. A RACI nobody has agreed to is a wish list.

Pair your RACI with a stakeholder map to decide how often each person hears from you. See more in stakeholder management.

Frequently asked questions

What does RACI stand for?

Responsible (does the work), Accountable (owns the outcome and signs off — exactly one per task), Consulted (gives input before the work is done; two-way) and Informed (kept up to date; one-way).

Can one person be both Responsible and Accountable?

Yes — that is common for smaller tasks and is shown as A/R.

What are RACI alternatives?

RASCI adds Support; DACI (Driver, Approver, Contributor, Informed) is popular for decisions; RAPID is used for complex organisational decisions.
