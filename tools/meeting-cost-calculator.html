Six ways to cut meeting cost without losing alignment

  1. Default to 25 or 50 minutes instead of 30 or 60.
  2. Publish an agenda with a decision to be made — no agenda, no meeting.
  3. Invite decision-makers; send notes to everyone else.
  4. Replace status meetings with a written status report.
  5. Use a parking lot for off-topic items.
  6. Review recurring meetings each quarter and cancel the ones nobody would miss.
