How to write a good risk statement

Use cause → risk → effect: "Because the vendor's API is still in beta (cause), it may change without notice (risk), which would break checkout and delay launch by 2–4 weeks (effect)." Vague risks such as "resources" or "budget" cannot be scored or owned.

Probability and impact scales

ScoreProbabilityImpact (example)
1Rare (<10%)Negligible; absorbed by the team
2Unlikely (10–30%)Minor slip, <2% budget
3Possible (30–50%)Milestone moves, 2–5% budget
4Likely (50–80%)Key deliverable late, 5–10% budget
5Almost certain (>80%)Objectives fail, >10% budget

Calibrate impact to your project — a 2% overrun on a $10M programme is not the same as on a $20k website. More in the project risk management guide.

Frequently asked questions

How is the risk score calculated?

Score = probability (1–5) × impact (1–5), giving 1 to 25. 15–25 is Critical, 10–14 High, 5–9 Medium and 1–4 Low.

What are the risk response strategies?

For threats: avoid, mitigate, transfer, accept or escalate. For opportunities: exploit, enhance, share, accept or escalate.

How often should I review the risk register?

At least every two weeks, and at every phase gate. Review high and critical risks weekly.
