Privacy Policy

Plain-English summary: tool data stays in your browser; form submissions are used only to respond to you; Google AdSense uses cookies to show ads.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

1. Who we are

Project.Management ("we", "us") operates this website. You can reach us through our contact form.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you submit through forms (contact, expert help, newsletter, toolkit, support, advertising, careers and contest forms): typically your name, email address and the details you choose to provide.
  • Tool data you enter in our tools (tasks, risks, budgets, etc.) is stored only in your own browser's local storage. It is not sent to us.
  • Usage data collected automatically by our hosting provider and advertising partners, such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and referring site.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiries and requests and, where you ask us to, introduce you to relevant specialists.
  • To send newsletters or course emails you sign up for (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To administer contests, careers applications and contributions.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To show advertising that helps keep the site free.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland may be shown a consent message managed through a Google-certified consent management platform, where required.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use essential local storage to remember your preferences (theme, cookie choice) and tool data. Advertising partners may set cookies as described above. See our Cookie Policy.

6. Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Once played, YouTube (Google) may collect data according to its own privacy policy.

7. Service providers

Form submissions are delivered to us through a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit). Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers process data on our behalf and under their own security measures.

8. Retention

We keep inquiry emails for up to 24 months, careers and talent-pool data for 12 months, and newsletter data until you unsubscribe, unless a longer period is required by law.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR and California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, use the contact form and choose "Privacy request". You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Children

This site is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Contests are open only to people aged 18 or over.

11. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted by the "Last updated" date above.
