Our mission

About Project.Management

Make good project management accessible to everyone — with tools that work instantly, guides that respect your time, and advice that isn't for sale.

Why we exist

Most "free" project management tools on the web are trials in disguise: you sign up, hand over your email, and the real features sit behind a paywall. Meanwhile, the people who most need help — new project managers, small businesses, non-profits and students — just need a Gantt chart, a risk register or a clear explanation of earned value, right now.

Project.Management is our answer: a growing library of browser-based tools that need no account, keep your data on your own device and export to the formats you already use; plain-English guides written by practitioners; and a vendor-neutral way to choose software and find expert help.

What we offer

  • 12 free tools — Gantt, Kanban, critical path, PERT, earned value, risk, RACI, charter, stakeholder map, sprint capacity, meeting cost and a methodology quiz.
  • 13 in-depth guides and a 150+ term glossary.
  • 12 downloadable templates with no email wall.
  • Software comparison and matcher with transparent scoring.
  • Free expert consultation for software, consultants, hiring and training.

Editorial standards

  • Original content. Everything is written for this site; we cite sources where we rely on them.
  • Independence. No vendor can pay to change our rankings or matcher scores. Sponsored placements are always labelled.
  • Accuracy. Certification requirements and software plans change; we date our reviews and link to official sources. Report an error and we'll fix it.
  • Privacy. Tool data stays in your browser. See our Privacy Policy.

How we're funded

Advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, optional reader support, and introductions to vetted partners when you ask for expert help. That mix lets us keep every tool free.
