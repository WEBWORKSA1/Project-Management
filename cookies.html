Cookie Policy

What we store on your device, why, and how to control it.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

What are cookies and local storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites. Local storage is a similar browser feature that keeps data on your device. We use both.

What we use

Name / typePurposeSet byDuration
pm-theme, pm-cookie, pm-sticky-x (local storage)Remember your theme, cookie choice and dismissed bannersUsUntil cleared
pm-gantt-v1, pm-kanban-v1, pm-risk-v1 and other pm-* keys (local storage)Save your tool data on your deviceUsUntil cleared
Advertising cookies (e.g. __gads, __gpi, IDE)Serve, measure and personalise ads; frequency capping; fraud preventionGoogle and partnersUp to 13 months
YouTube cookiesVideo playback (only after you press play)Google / YouTubeVaries

Your choices

  • Use the cookie banner to accept all or essential-only cookies.
  • Manage personalised ads at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Clearing local storage will delete your saved tool data, so export it first.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
