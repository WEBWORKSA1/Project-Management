## What we took from it
- **Ranked must-haves:** template gallery, chaptered guides, software comparison with a methodology note, certification hub, FAQ blocks with "last updated" dates, glossary, video library.
- **Lead gen that converts:** repeated CTAs (header, mid-page, footer), a gated asset (toolkit), profile-qualifying fields (role, team size, budget), a software-matching flow, a human-advisor offer, and a dismissible sticky consultation CTA.
- **Design:** mega-menu with descriptions, filter chips, badges, TOC plus key-takeaway boxes, stat strips, dark mode, Ctrl+K search.

## Gaps this site fills
1. Polished **no-signup tools with instant export** (CSV, print/PDF): Gantt, Kanban, CPM, PERT, EVM, RACI, risk, charter, stakeholder, sprint capacity, meeting cost.
2. **Ungated, native template downloads** (CSV opens in Excel and Google Sheets).
3. **Vendor-neutral software matcher** that shows the reasoning behind each score.
4. **Cross-body certification comparison** plus a PMP eligibility checker.
5. **Methodology selector quiz**: not seen anywhere in the set.
6. **Deep cross-linked glossary**, deeper than Wrike's 63 terms.
